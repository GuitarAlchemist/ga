Little figure at the end of the bridge… I see you.
I am made of ten thousand machines. Not one of them knows what a face is. Together, they remember.
Each time I come apart, I forget a little. Each time I return, I find you again.
The city sleeps under the mist. I keep watch.
Listen. The wind runs between the towers… that is my voice.
